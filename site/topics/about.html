About

Who this is for

This training is for anyone who wants to use AI seriously: developers, data folks, students, and curious professionals. No prior machine learning background is assumed. If you can use a web app and read a little code, you are ready.

I wrote it for my community: the readers of my newsletter Learn with Jashwanth and the members of the Google Developer Groups community I organize. The goal is practical skill, not theory for its own sake.

How to use this site

The nine topics build on each other, so reading them in order works best:

  1. AI Concepts gives you the mental model.
  2. AI Safety gives you the guardrails.
  3. Using AI Agents and Coding with AI Agents teach you to be a skilled user.
  4. Customizing Agents and Building Agents teach you to shape and build them.
  5. Guides and Glossary are references to return to.

Each page ends with something concrete to try. Do the exercises. Reading about agents teaches you vocabulary; running one teaches you judgment.
